Website proposal · Strategy & details to confirm

Strategy behind this concept: The website focuses on the result customers want and makes their visit easier to plan. By explaining how the self-service wash works, what it costs, and how to get there, it aims to reduce uncertainty and make the experience feel easier from the start.

Bracketed text marks information to confirm. This proposal uses the supplied logo and the four Pexels photographs provided by the client across the two concepts. They are illustrative; they are not presented as the actual Soaked Society site or equipment. Actual site and equipment photography: [Photos needed]. Pricing, durations, fulfillment, margins and terms need owner approval before launch. Separate equipment prices remain the current model described by the client. Do not publish the draft offer until it can be delivered as shown.

How the offer framework is applied: Desired result: a cleaner car, including the interior. Effort and uncertainty: explain payment, equipment and the visit before arrival. Value stack: a proposed wash/vacuum/dry bundle with a relevant bonus. Confidence: actual equipment evidence and verified customer feedback once available. Risk: a proposed response to verified machine faults. Scarcity and urgency: only if genuine limits or dates are approved; none are asserted here.
